AM Tower Locator (Mobile)

Tap anywhere on the map or use GPS to set your reference point. Select a radius to display nearby AM transmitter sites.

Tap a station dot to see its callsign, frequency, operating status, power, distance, and bearing.

Turn off Current only to include historical, inactive, and superseded database records. GPS is used once when requested; it does not continuously track your position.

Results are derived from FCC LMS data and Canadian ISED status information. Records may contain errors.
