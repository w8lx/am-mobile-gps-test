v1.0 - Debugging

Select a frequency, then Active Day, Active Night, All, or Live DX to plot antenna patterns on a common map.

Active Day and Active Night use current licensing overlays for U.S., Canadian, and Mexican stations. All includes historical and superseded database records.

Patterns are normalized for comparison. Their displayed size does not represent field strength, coverage, or licensed contour distance. Tap a pattern or site for station details. Scale adjusts the relative display size of directional and nondirectional patterns. Live DX follows the current UTC time and solar terminator at each transmitter; use the time slider to explore other UTC times, or Now to resume live time. It does not use authorized switching times.
